The word lists in this directory (*.deflate) are derived from FrequencyWords
by Hermit Dave (https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords), built from the
OpenSubtitles 2018 corpus (http://opus.nlpl.eu/OpenSubtitles2018.php).

They are licensed under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0
International (CC BY-SA 4.0): https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

Changes: filtered to lowercase words of three or more letters, top 50,000
(Russian) and 30,000 (English) by frequency, sorted and compressed.
Built with scripts/build_dict_packs.py.

This license covers only the word lists. RuSwitcher itself is MIT-licensed
and does not bundle these files; they are downloaded on request.
